Boat and island realism pass
============================

The website loads the `*-realistic.glb` assets. Original models and construction
sources from before this pass are preserved in `backups/before-realism/`.

Boat: unlettered transom, fine timber grain, narrower boot stripe, lighter stitched
canvas with tension wrinkles, revised lifelines, coiled halyards, winch grooves,
fastened deck hardware and a stowed anchor. The body has a 4096px PBR atlas;
each animated sail has a 2048px atlas. Billow/Flutter morphs, flag, rudder, tiller
and lantern remain separate. Browser materials add restrained varnish and cloth
sheen. The water cutout follows the hull section at the current wave height.

Outpost: paler, less exaggerated sandstone, finer normal detail, 4096px PBR atlas.
City: actual curtain-wall mullions and floor spandrels, less metallic wall panels,
roof plant, and quieter window emission. Carnival: weathered painted steel and
finer wheel atlases; live tent roofs have cloth sag and a subtle weave normal.

Island terrain remains procedural in `components/world/Islands.tsx`, allowing
shore foam, rocks and props to use the exact same ground-height functions. The
terrain now has broader bedrock caps, a full foundation under the fortress,
fractured rather than spherical shore rocks, finer meshes and muted vegetation.
Its material layers are baked in Blender by `coastal_materials.py`: four-metre
seamless sand, stone, soil and concrete surfaces, with color/normal/roughness maps.
Triplanar mapping in `components/world/coastal.ts` avoids stretched vertical cliffs.
Sand blends onto flatter desert shelves and low beaches; wetness darkens and
smooths stone at the tide line. Mobile retains smaller terrain grids.

Rebuild models from the project root, using the installed Blender executable:

    blender --background --python blender/boat.py -- public/models/boat-realistic.glb blender/boat-realistic.blend
    blender --background --python blender/outpost.py -- public/models/outpost-realistic.glb blender/outpost-realistic.blend
    blender --background --python blender/city.py -- public/models/city-realistic.glb blender/city-realistic.blend
    blender --background --python blender/wheel.py -- public/models/wheel-realistic.glb blender/wheel-realistic.blend
    blender --background --python blender/coastal_materials.py

The packed `*-realistic.blend` previews are re-imports of the shipping GLBs with
their embedded textures and a daylight camera, generated by
`pack_realistic_sources.py`. The construction Python scripts remain the procedural
source of truth. `lib.save_blend` packs generated images on subsequent rebuilds.

Validate mesh animation contracts and embedded textures:

    node blender/verify_realistic_assets.cjs

TypeScript and the optimized Next.js build were checked; the live scene is also
checked in the browser because shader compilation is a separate runtime step.

## Terrain detail pass
Coastal surfaces are now baked at 2048px in Blender, with fractured rock normals and stronger fine relief. Terrain uses slope-aware sediment layers, denser meshes, grounded shoreline scatter and instanced coastal grass. Rebuild the maps with coastal_materials.py. Island chapter text uses transparent overlays with a continuous gradient for readability; Earth uses a low-strength textured fill so its shadowed continents remain visible.
