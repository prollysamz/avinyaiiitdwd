## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes the grandeur of large-format editorial print, high-drama cinematic title sequences, and the obsessive precision of 18th-century nautical charts and deep-orbital navigational logs. It balances sweeping scale against microscopic instrumentation: monumental typography anchors wide expanses of negative space, while pinpoint telemetry, coordinates, and crosshair registers trace the edges of the frame.

The emotional tone is epic, contemplative, and rigorously structured. Users interact not as passive spectators, but as navigators decoding uncharted sectors—from desert plateaus and neon river deltas to incandescent night harbors and cold orbital voids.

### Visual Style
- **Cinematic Editorial & Technical Brutalism:** Extreme typographic scale shifts, razor-sharp 90-degree geometry, ultra-fine coordinate grids, and monospaced telemetry annotations.
- **Surface Depth:** Pure pitch-black voids layered with subtle ink washes, glowing coordinates, and targeted chromatic bursts tied directly to distinct planetary sectors.
- **Tactile Nautical Instrumentation:** Lat/long coordinate locks, sector indexes, crosshair markers, and index callouts inspired by astronomical logbooks and archival map plates.

## Colors

The foundation is anchored in deep orbital darkness (`#07090D`), paired with structural off-whites and tinted bone tones for crisp editorial legibility. Color is applied with deliberate spatial intent, segmented into regional palettes representing four uncharted territories:

### System & Framework Anchors
- **Abyssal Base (Neutral):** `#07090D` (Canvas foundation)
- **Deep Hull Tint:** `#0E121A` (Card / structural background)
- **Surface Elevation:** `#171D26` (Panel & overlay fill)
- **Grid / Hairline Rule:** `rgba(235, 240, 245, 0.08)`
- **Celestial Bone (Primary Text):** `#F0F2F5`
- **Telemetry Muted (Secondary Text):** `#7E8B9B`

### Sector Palettes (Territories)
- **Sector I: Desert Rust & Dune:** `#E05338` (Terra Cotta), `#C87A3E` (Burnt Ochre), `#F4D399` (Bleached Sand)
- **Sector II: Synthwave Delta:** `#FF007A` (Hyper Magenta), `#8B00FF` (Electric Violet), `#00F0FF` (Bioluminescent Cyan)
- **Sector III: Carnival Harbor:** `#FFB703` (Lantern Gold), `#D90429` (Festival Crimson), `#FB8500` (Amber Flare)
- **Sector IV: Hack2Future Orbital:** `#00E5FF` (Orbital Cyan), `#FF5500` (Warning Orange), `#F8F9FA` (Cold Starlight)

High-chroma accents should remain sparse—acting as active waypoints, focal telemetry points, and energetic horizon lines rather than broad flat fills.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy relies on dramatic tension between three distinct families:
1. **The Editorial Serif (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers towering cinematic scale, razor-thin hairlines, and opulent serifs reminiscent of oversized titles and historical maritime folios.
2. **The Modern Humanist (`DM Sans`):** Grounds reading flow with high legibility, balanced proportions, and clean modern neutralism for body narrative and log text.
3. **The Precision Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`):** Acts as the cartographic ledger—handling coordinates, timecodes, sector classifications, and navigational bearings. All labels, tags, and data values default to uppercase in this face.

Display headlines should comfortably span viewport widths, utilizing tight negative tracking to maintain punch.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout adheres to an architectural cartographic grid:
- **Desktop:** 12-column fluid grid framed inside 40px (`2.5rem`) canvas margins. Subdivided by fine visible or implied hairline coordinate axes.
- **Tablet:** 8 columns, 32px margins, 20px gutters.
- **Mobile:** 4 columns, 16px margins, 12px gutters.

### Editorial Composition Rules
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Hero typographic elements intentionally bleed over hairline dividers or align flush to the left boundary while telemetry data runs vertically along the right perimeter.
- **Reticule Spacing:** Every module or card boundary reserves dedicated clearance (`space-md`) for corner registration marks (`+`, `L-brackets`, or micro-coordinates).
- **Edge Running Telemetry:** Margins frequently house continuous running headers and footers containing log coordinates, bearing indicators, and page sector numbers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on soft, blotted drop shadows. Depth is achieved strictly through:
- **Low-Contrast Hairline Framing:** Crisp 1px borders (`rgba(235, 240, 245, 0.12)`) establish structural compartments, evoking draftsmanship diagrams.
- **Tonal Substrates:** Stacking panels using slight opacity variations of midnight black and cold ink slate (`#07090D` to `#0E121A` to `#171D26`).
- **Luminescent Back-Glows:** Key interactive elements and sector cores emit tight, high-intensity radial halos (e.g., `0 0 24px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)` or `0 0 32px rgba(255, 0, 122, 0.3)`), mimicking navigational beacons emerging through planetary haze.

## Shapes

The geometry is strictly **Sharp (0px)** across the entire system. 

- **No border radii:** Buttons, inputs, modal containers, and badges feature unyielding 90-degree right angles.
- **Chamfers and Cut Corners:** Selected primary headers and actionable tiles may incorporate 45-degree corner notches (4px to 8px chamfers) via CSS clip-paths to emphasize naval chart drafting and scientific instruments.
- **Structural Framing:** Dividers terminate in perpendicular tick marks or crosshairs.

## Components

### Buttons & Navigational Triggers
- **Primary:** Sharp black background framed by a 1px border colored with the active territory's accent (e.g., `#00F0FF` or `#E05338`). Text rendered in uppercase `JetBrains Mono` with wide tracking. On hover, fills solid with accent color while text shifts to pure `#07090D`.
- **Ghost / Telemetry Button:** Borderless, framed only by left and right tick brackets (`[ ENTER LOG ]`), glowing subtly on focus.

### Cards & Log Enclosures
- Flat `#0E121A` fill enclosed in a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Card headers incorporate an index strip: sector number on the left (`SEC_01 // DESERT`), lat/long tracking on the right.
- Distinct corner reticules (`+`) anchored to top-left and bottom-right bounds.

### Input Fields
- Monospaced inputs set against `#07090D` with an underline-only or 1px sharp perimeter border.
- Active focus state features an accent-colored border accompanied by an unfurling coordinate cursor.
- Monospaced prompt labels positioned flush above with metadata brackets.

### Chips & Coordinate Tags
- Compact, sharp rectangles with 1px border.
- Monospace micro typography (`telemetry-micro`).
- Sector indicator dots (3px square) preceding the status text.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Binary square checkboxes (14px × 14px), 0px radius.
- Checked state fills with a high-contrast sector glyph (`X` or solid inner square).
- Segmented switches operate as contiguous modular bar tabs divided by 1px vertical hairlines.

### Sector Radar & Cartographic Compass (Specialty Component)
- Decorative hairline reticules overlaid with rotating orbital rings, cardinal ticks (`N`, `S`, `E`, `W`), and continuous numeric degree readouts.
